- Poursalehian et al., PLOS ONE 2024 (35 studies, n=1107): no sig change T3/T4/TSH/Testosterone/LH/FSH/Prolactin; morning cortisol trend ↓ (p=0.08), evening ↑ circadian shift; safe for healthy. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0299695
- Al-Rawi et al., PLOS ONE 2020 (n=57 overweight/obese): ghrelin -19%, leptin -13%, melatonin -39%, cortisol unchanged; weight/BMI/fat ↓ HDL↑. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0237922
