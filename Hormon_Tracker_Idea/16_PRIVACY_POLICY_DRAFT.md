## EN

**Hormon Tracker — Privacy Policy (v1.0)**

1. **Guest-first.** The app works fully without an account. Guest data (check-ins, estimates, preferences) stays in this device only.
2. **Health data never leaves your device.** Sleep, steps and workouts are read with your permission, summarized on-device into daily estimates, and never uploaded — even if you create an account.
3. **Optional account.** Login (Apple/Google/Email) exists only for encrypted backup and multi-device sync. Synced: profile settings, manual inputs, daily estimates. Never synced: raw health samples.
4. **No selling, no ads, no supplement marketing.** Analytics (if enabled) are anonymous counts only — never health values.
5. **Your rights.** Export your data (Settings → Export), erase device data, delete your cloud account anytime (Settings → Delete account, 30-day grace).
6. **Children.** Under 13: guest mode only, no account, no cloud.
7. **Medical.** Estimates are educational trends, not lab measurements or diagnoses.
